Programs

A Program for Every Stage of Early Childhood

From a child's very first hours away from home to the year before primary school — each stage has its own curriculum, pace, and classroom setup.

1.5 – 2.5 Years

Play Group

The gentlest introduction to school life. Short hours, high adult attention, and a focus on comfort, sensory play, and simple routines that help children separate from parents with confidence.

  • Sensory & motor-skill play
  • Music, rhymes and free movement
  • Low teacher-to-child ratio for individual attention
2.5 – 3.5 Years

Pre-KG

Structured play begins here. Children start recognising shapes, colours, and letters through games, not worksheets, while building the social skills needed for a classroom setting.

  • Phonics through stories and songs
  • Fine motor skills — tracing, threading, building
  • Group activities to build sharing and turn-taking
3.5 – 4.5 Years

LKG

A confident step into academics. Reading readiness, early number sense, and independent thinking are introduced through hands-on activities and guided exploration.

  • Early literacy and number concepts
  • Show-and-tell to build spoken confidence
  • Art, craft and simple science exploration
4.5 – 5.5 Years

UKG

Preparation for primary school, without losing the joy of learning. Reading fluency, writing, and basic math are built alongside responsibility, focus, and classroom discipline.

  • Reading fluency and guided writing
  • Foundational math and logical thinking
  • School-readiness routines and independence
All Ages · Extended Hours

Day Care

For working parents who need trustworthy care beyond school hours. Supervised meals, rest time, homework support for older children, and the same warm caregivers your child already knows.

  • Flexible pick-up windows
  • Supervised rest and homework time
  • Same campus, same familiar teachers

How We Teach

The method behind every program, whatever the age group.

Play-Based Learning

Concepts are taught through games and stories, not rote memorisation.

Small Group Sizes

Low child-to-teacher ratios so no child is left behind or overlooked.

Parent Partnership

Regular updates and open conversations, not just a report card at year-end.
